1. Executive Case Summary & Decision Rationale EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This Statutory Investigation Brief is issued for target project LS-EXP-2024-8842 ("Construction of Boundary Wall & Interlocking Pavers at Community Center, Badagaon"). The project demonstrates serious statutory non-compliance, resulting in a low Compliance & Health Score of 14.2 / 100. Key observations indicate premature drawdown of 99.1% of sanctioned funds against only 35.0% verified physical completion, systematic splitting of payment vouchers to bypass approval thresholds, and vendor cartelization in the district.

INTERIM STATUTORY ENFORCEMENT ORDER:
Pursuant to executive powers under Section 11.2 of the MPLADS Operational Guidelines read with General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 Rule 211, all pending and future milestone disbursements to Implementing Agency Rural Engineering Department (RED Div-II) and Contractor M/s Ajay Kumar Singh Construction under CNA Account #UP-VAR-091 are hereby FROZEN WITH IMMEDIATE EFFECT pending formal audit reconciliation.

2. Detected Irregularities & Core Reasoning FINDINGS & REASONING

Key operational findings with plain-language regulatory reasoning:

FINDING 1 ARTIFICIAL PAYMENT FRAGMENTATION (TENDER SPLITTING)
GFR 2017 Rule 157 & 149(i) CRITICAL
Mandated Policy
Competitive e-procurement through open tenders or GeM portal is mandatory for cumulative civil work contracts. Dividing contracts into small chunks to avoid approvals is prohibited.
Observed Fact
52 separate payment vouchers were issued between ₹19,900 and ₹19,995 across 43 days, totaling ₹23,79,048 to the same contractor.
Reasoning & Impact
Reasoning: Payments were deliberately kept ₹8 below the ₹20,000 threshold to evade mandatory higher-tier executive sign-offs and open competitive bidding.
Impact: Public funds were disbursed without competitive rate discovery or supervisory verification.
LEDGER: VCH-2024-001 to 052
FINDING 2 PREMATURE FUND RELEASE WITHOUT ON-SITE CONSTRUCTION
MPLADS Guidelines §3.4 CRITICAL
Mandated Policy
Fund releases must correspond strictly with verified on-ground completion. For foundation work, admissible payout cannot exceed 35% (₹8,40,000).
Observed Fact
99.1% of sanctioned allocation (₹23.79 Lakhs) has been drawn down, while independent physical inspection verifies only 35.0% completion.
Reasoning & Impact
Reasoning: Implementing agency paid out full project value before the brick boundary walls and paver blocks were even commenced on site.
Impact: ₹15.39 Lakhs in public capital has been given as an unauthorized cash advance to the contractor without asset backing.
INSPECTION REPORT: FVO-UP-041
FINDING 3 EXCESSIVE VENDOR CONCENTRATION & CARTELIZATION
Competition Act 2002 §3 • GFR Rule 144 HIGH
Mandated Policy
Fair and transparent public procurement requires non-discriminatory participation. Single vendor concentration should not corner a parliamentary jurisdiction.
Observed Fact
A single contractor was awarded 119 out of 134 civil works (88.4% of total parliamentary constituency value, ₹2.84 Cr).
Reasoning & Impact
Reasoning: Evidence points to exclusionary bidding practices that systematically favor one vendor over all local competing firms.
Impact: Eliminates competitive cost pressure and indicates potential administrative favoritism.
GSTIN: 09AAACA0123M1Z5
FINDING 4 STATUTORY UTILIZATION CERTIFICATE (GFR 12-A) DEFAULT
GFR 2017 Rule 238(1) HIGH
